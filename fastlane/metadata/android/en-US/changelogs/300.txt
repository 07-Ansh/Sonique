Sonique v3.0.0

What's New:
• Library & Albums: Dedicated Albums tab with a 3-column ambient grid view, custom local playlist creation, rename/delete capabilities, and Downloaded Songs quick access.
• Audio Engine: Smart DJ crossfade with configurable duration, dynamic audio volume normalization, and Media3 1.10.1 backend update.
• Android Auto: Added Recents tab to car dashboard, improved MediaSession steering wheel controls, and full queue browsing.
• In-App Updates: Direct release checking, changelog preview, and APK downloading directly within settings.
• Design & UX: Material 3 Expressive components, dynamic Download artwork tiles, and spring physics press micro-animations.

Fixes & Performance:
• Fixed mini player and dock collapse behavior on Search screen.
• Fixed local playlist filtering and Quick Access state sync.
• Optimized release builds with R8 minification, resource shrinking, and ABI splits.
