Sonique v3.0.0 — Major Update! 🎉

✨ What's New:
• Seamless Audio Playback: Overhauled player engine for smooth, continuous, gapless music playback without interruptions.
• Smart Audio Controls: Added DJ-style track crossfading and automatic volume balancing across quiet and loud songs.
• Synced Lyrics: Real-time, line-by-line synchronized lyrics with interactive seeking — tap any line to jump right to it.
• Dedicated Albums & Playlists: Brand new Albums section in your library, plus full support for creating, renaming, and managing offline playlists.
• Android Auto: Access your recently played tracks directly from your car's dashboard with full steering wheel controls.
• Built-In App Updates: Check for new releases and update Sonique directly from the app settings.

⚡ Improvements & Fixes:
• Fixed playback stopping randomly between songs for a completely uninterrupted listening experience.
• Fixed mini-player and bottom navigation dock behavior on the Search screen.
• Improved offline downloads stability and overall app speed.
