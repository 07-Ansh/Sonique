Sonique is a modern, minimal, and ad-free music streaming app for Android, powered by YouTube Music. Built with Jetpack Compose and Kotlin Multiplatform.

<b>Core Music &amp; Playback</b>
• 100% ad-free streaming with zero audio interruptions
• Built-in SponsorBlock to automatically skip non-music segments and intros
• Seamless background playback with screen-off support
• Smart audio crossfade for gap-free listening between tracks
• Dynamic volume normalization across all songs
• Offline downloads for songs, albums, and full playlists

<b>Synced Lyrics</b>
• Real-time line-by-line synchronized lyrics powered by 7 providers (LyricsPlus, LRCLIB, KuGou, BetterLyrics, Paxsenix, and YouTube)
• Tap any lyric line to jump playback to that exact moment

<b>Library &amp; Playlist Management</b>
• Create, rename, and organize custom local playlists on your device
• Optional YouTube Music sign-in to sync existing playlists, favorites, and mixes

<b>Connected &amp; Smart Features</b>
• Full Android Auto integration for in-car listening
• Bedtime sleep timer with automatic playback pause
• One-tap in-app updates directly from the app settings
• Multi-language support for 25+ languages

<b>Privacy &amp; Control</b>
• Zero analytics, no trackers, no third-party telemetry
• All data stored locally on your device
• Optional opt-in listening history sync with YouTube Music
• Content filtering to skip off-topic music segments
