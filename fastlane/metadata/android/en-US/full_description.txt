Sonique is a modern, ad-free music player built with Material 3 design, offering seamless playback, synchronized lyrics, and offline downloads.

<b>Core Experience</b>
• <b>Ad-Free Playback:</b> Uninterrupted listening with zero ads or audio breaks.
• <b>Smart Segment Skipping:</b> Integrated SponsorBlock skips non-music intros and outros.
• <b>Background Playback:</b> Keep listening while using other apps or with your screen turned off.
• <b>Offline Downloads:</b> Save tracks, albums, and playlists directly to your device.

<b>Audio &amp; Tuning</b>
• <b>Audio Crossfade:</b> Smooth track transitions without silences between songs.
• <b>Volume Normalization:</b> Consistent sound levels across quiet and loud tracks.

<b>Lyrics &amp; Discovery</b>
• <b>Real-Time Synced Lyrics:</b> Line-by-line synchronized lyrics powered by multi-engine fallback.
• <b>Interactive Seeking:</b> Tap any lyric line to jump playback directly to that moment.

<b>Connected &amp; Smart</b>
• <b>Android Auto:</b> Full dashboard integration for in-car listening.
• <b>Sleep Timer:</b> Pauses playback automatically when you fall asleep.
• <b>YouTube Sync:</b> Optional sign-in to access your existing playlists and mixes.

<b>Privacy &amp; Control</b>
• Zero analytics, telemetry, or user tracking.
• Local-first data architecture for privacy.
